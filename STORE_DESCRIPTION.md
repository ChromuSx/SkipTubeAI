SAVE PRECIOUS TIME ON YOUTUBE WITH SKIPTUBE AI!

Have you ever wished you could automatically skip sponsorships, long intros, and promotional content in YouTube videos? SkipTube AI is the solution you've been looking for! This Chrome extension uses advanced AI — your choice of Claude (by Anthropic) or OpenAI GPT — to automatically analyze videos and precisely identify the segments you likely want to skip.


🚀 KEY FEATURES

🤖 ADVANCED ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
• Analyzes video transcripts with state-of-the-art AI
• Accurately identifies sponsors, intros, outros, and promotional content
• Choose your provider and model:
  – Claude (Anthropic): Haiku (fast & economical) or Sonnet (more accurate)
  – OpenAI: GPT-5.5 (best), GPT-5.4 Mini (fast), or GPT-5.4 Nano (cheapest)

🛡️ RESILIENT TO YOUTUBE CHANGES
• Reads YouTube's own transcript data instead of fragile page elements
• When YouTube redesigns its layout, the extension adapts automatically using AI — re-learning how to read the transcript on its own
• Keeps working through YouTube updates that break other extensions

⚡ AUTOMATIC SKIPPING
• Automatically skips identified segments without interruption
• Configurable buffer for a smooth viewing experience
• Preview notifications before skipping (optional)

🎨 VISUAL TIMELINE MARKERS
• View identified segments directly on YouTube's progress bar
• Color-coded by category (red=sponsor, orange=self-promo, cyan=intro, purple=outro, green=donations)
• Clickable to manually jump to any segment

⚙️ FULLY CUSTOMIZABLE
• Choose which categories to skip (sponsors, intros, outros, donations, self-promotion)
• Adjust AI confidence threshold (from 0.5 to 1.0)
• Configure skip buffer (from 0 to 3 seconds)
• Exclude specific channels from automatic analysis

💾 INTELLIGENT CACHING
• Analysis results are saved locally for 30 days
• No need to re-analyze the same video multiple times
• Saves API calls and loading time

📊 DETAILED STATISTICS
• View how much time you've saved
• Track skipped segments and analyzed videos
• Easily manage your analyzed video cache

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Use YOUR personal API key (Anthropic or OpenAI)
• All data is stored locally on your device
• No tracking, no personal data collection
• Open source code verifiable on GitHub


🎬 HOW IT WORKS

1. INITIAL SETUP (ONE TIME)
   • Get an API key from your preferred provider — Anthropic (https://console.anthropic.com) or OpenAI (https://platform.openai.com/api-keys)
   • Select your provider and enter the key in the extension settings
   • Choose the content categories you want to skip

2. DURING PLAYBACK
   • When you open a YouTube video with captions, the extension automatically extracts the transcript
   • Your chosen AI analyzes the content and identifies segments to skip
   • Results are saved in local cache for future use
   • Colored markers appear on the video timeline
   • The extension automatically skips segments during playback

3. MANAGEMENT
   • View statistics in the extension popup
   • Manage your analyzed video cache
   • Modify settings anytime
   • Exclude specific channels to support your favorite creators

🔧 REQUIREMENTS & SETUP

REQUIREMENTS:
• Google Chrome (or Chromium-based browser)
• An AI API key — Anthropic Claude (console.anthropic.com) or OpenAI (platform.openai.com)
• YouTube videos with available captions/transcripts

SETUP (2 MINUTES):
1. Install the extension from Chrome Web Store
2. Click the extension icon in the toolbar
3. Choose your AI provider and enter its API key in the "API Configuration" section
4. Click "Save" - done! The extension is ready to use

The extension uses YOUR own API key (Anthropic or OpenAI), so you pay your provider directly for usage — there is no subscription to us. Thanks to intelligent caching, each video is analyzed ONLY ONCE, keeping usage to a minimum.

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Does the extension work on all YouTube videos?
A: It works on all videos that have available captions/transcripts. If a video doesn't have transcripts, the extension cannot analyze it.

Q: Do I have to pay to use the extension?
A: The extension is free. You only pay your AI provider for the analysis, and thanks to caching, each video is analyzed only once.

Q: Which AI provider should I choose?
A: Both work great. Claude Haiku and OpenAI's Mini/Nano models are fast and economical; Claude Sonnet and GPT-5.5 are more accurate for tricky videos. You can switch anytime in the settings.

Q: Is my API key secure?
A: Yes! Your API key is stored locally on your device using Chrome's secure storage API. It's never transmitted to us or to third parties (except your chosen provider, Anthropic or OpenAI, for analysis).

Q: What happens when YouTube changes its design?
A: SkipTube AI primarily reads YouTube's own transcript data, which is far more stable than page elements. If a redesign does break extraction, the extension uses AI to automatically figure out the new layout and keep working — no update required on your part.

Q: Can I exclude certain channels?
A: Absolutely! Go to "Advanced Settings" and add channels to the whitelist. Perfect for supporting your favorite creators.

Q: Does the extension slow down YouTube?
A: No! Analysis happens in the background and caching ensures already-analyzed videos load instantly (< 100ms).

Q: Can I customize what gets skipped?
A: Yes! You can individually enable/disable each category (sponsors, intros, outros, donations, self-promotion). You can also adjust the AI confidence threshold.

Q: Does the extension work offline?
A: Already-analyzed videos (in cache) will be skipped even offline. Analyzing new videos requires an internet connection.

Q: Can I see details of identified segments?
A: Yes! Click the "View cache" button in the popup to see all analyzed videos with detailed segment information.
